Head Coach Quick Start

Set up your program once. Parent coaches should only need the match-day scorecard and permitted lineup changes.

  1. Create divisions (Orange Ball, Green Ball, etc.).
  2. Add teams and athletes to each division.
  3. Assign parent coaches and choose their lineup permissions.
  4. Plan a match: division, team, date, opponent, format, start time and location. Choose the lineup and save.
  5. Use Share Lineup by Text to open your phone's group-text share menu. Optional Snacks/Drinks and the 30-minute warm-up reminder are included.
  6. On match day, open Match Day, choose the correct scorecard and load the saved lineup by date.

Important: This is a local-device test build. It does not yet provide secure coach sign-in, synchronized permissions, shared live links or automatic group notifications. Sending the text does not grant app access.
