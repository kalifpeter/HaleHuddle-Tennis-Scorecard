Junior Timed Match

For Orange Ball or Green Ball timed team matches. Choose the number of singles and doubles courts you need, then run each court's timer and enter the final score.
Match Setup
Setup
Default is 2 singles + 2 doubles. Change the counts or match length, then tap Apply. Each court gets its own independent timer. When time expires, that court always shows TIME. Vibration depends on device/browser support and may not work on iPhone. Sound Alert uses three clear notification chimes. The timer starts silently; the chimes play only when time expires.
